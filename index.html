Barcode Tools

Decode Generate

Two small barcode utilities that run entirely client-side — no server, no upload, nothing leaves your browser.

Decode →

Upload an image and decode every barcode on it, including function characters (FNC1–4) and other non-printable payload bytes, via a WebAssembly build of zxing-cpp.

Generate →

A thin browser wrapper around BWIPP (via bwip-js) — pick a symbology, pass raw options, get a PNG or SVG.

Barcode decoding by zxing-cpp (via zxing-wasm). Barcode generation by BWIPP (via bwip-js).
